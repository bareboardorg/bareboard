    Exploded View: Permobil F3 Corpus

    This page is different, and the difference is the story. Permobil does not publish an Illustrated Parts Breakdown for the F3 Corpus — not for owners, not openly at all. Their user manual says it in one line: “Spare parts must be ordered through your dealer.” No exploded views, no part numbers, no way to order a caster bearing without a dealer in the middle.

    Coverage: manual-only. What Permobil does publish is the labeled overview in the manual — the whole chair, thirteen labeled parts. That is everything the maker gives the person who rides in it. We reproduce the diagram exactly, and we name the gap exactly: every assembly-level breakdown for this chair is a source gap until Permobil publishes one. The F3 Corpus is a front-wheel-drive power wheelchair for outdoor and indoor use, for people limited to a seated position. The manual gives its expected service life as five years.

    The sources

    Guide: User Manual — Permobil F3 Corpus, Model version 3, Edition 5, 2025-04-16, Order no. 340050 eng-US
    Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): permobil_f3-corpus_owner.pdf

    No IPB, parts catalogue, or service manual for the F3 Corpus is published openly by Permobil — their service manuals are written “for technical personnel” and distributed through dealers, and their documentation portal requires a login (verified 2026-11-12). A service manual for the F3 Corpus exists (version 3, 200 pages), but the only copy found openly is behind a free-account login on iFixit, so nothing was collected from it and no login was bypassed. If that changes, this page gets the full exploded treatment the same week.

    The maker’s own warnings, word for word

    Permobil prints these in the F3 Corpus user manual. Word for word — including the one that explains why this page cannot give you part numbers:

    Spare parts must be ordered through your dealer. Only use spare parts that are authorized by Permobil.

    IMPORTANT! Do not modify the wheelchair. Do not modify your wheelchair or any of its components. Your wheelchair has been configured specifically for your needs as prescribed by your healthcare provider. Special skills, training, and knowledge are needed to set up, modify, and repair the wheelchair.

    WARNING! Do not overload the wheelchair. Do not use the wheelchair to pull any kind of object and never hang excessive weights on the wheelchair. If you do, the wheelchair can become less stable and injure you.

    WARNING! Use freewheel mode cautiously. Freewheel mode should only be used on level ground and when an attendant is present to control the wheelchair. If you use freewheel mode on an incline or without an attendant, you can get injured or cause property damage.

    Verbatim from the Permobil F3 Corpus User Manual, sections 2.11 and 3.

    Build order

    The assemblies are laid out in the order you would build the chair: frame first, batteries last. Every step is one labeled diagram from the maker’s guide, followed by its parts table. The Callout column is the label printed on the diagram. There is no part-number column on this page — Permobil publishes no part numbers for this chair, and we do not invent them.

    • Frame: Step 1: The F3 Corpus, labeled by Permobil

    Stage 1 — Frame

    Start with the skeleton. Every other system bolts to the frame, so it goes together first.

    Step 1 — The F3 Corpus, labeled by Permobil

    Part (maker’s label)What this part does
    A. ArmrestSupports your arms, and helps you transfer in and out of the chair.
    B. Control panelThe hand control: joystick, buttons, and the display that shows battery level and speed.
    C. SeatThe seat you sit on, part of the Corpus seating system.
    D. LightA light on the chair, so you can be seen in the dark.
    E. Leg restSupports your legs. It can tilt to change their angle.
    F. Support wheelThe small wheel at the front that stops the chair tipping forward. Do not remove it.
    G. FootplateThe plate your feet rest on.
    H. HeadrestSupports your head.
    I. BackrestSupports your back.
    J. ChassisThe frame of the chair. Everything else bolts to it.
    K. Caster wheelThe small swivel wheel at the corner. It is not powered — it follows where the chair turns.
    L. Drive wheelThe big powered wheel. The motor turns this one.
    M. Serial number labelThe label with this chair’s serial number. Every parts and service conversation starts with this number — photograph yours now.

    What is missing, in the open

    Frame, drive, seating, and electronics breakdowns for this chair: not published by the maker. That is not a gap in our research — it is the gate, documented. A service manual for the F3 Corpus exists (version 3, 200 pages), but Permobil distributes service manuals through dealers and its documentation portal requires a login, and the only openly found copy sits behind a free-account login on iFixit, so no openly published copy is hosted here. The serial number label — callout M — is the one key you hold: every part on this chair is orderable by a dealer using it. Until Permobil publishes the breakdown, a dealer’s parts screen is the only copy — and anything a dealer can see for your chair, you are entitled to ask them to show you.
