DMCA Policy

How we handle copyright complaints. Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Our approach

BareBoard respects the intellectual property rights of others and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). We will respond to legitimate takedown notices and have a designated agent for receiving them.

We believe our content — educational teardown documentation, commentary, and reference archival of manufacturer-published technical documents — is consistent with fair use under 17 U.S.C. § 107. However, we take all good-faith complaints seriously and will review each one individually.

Designated DMCA agent

Notifications of claimed infringement should be sent to:

  • Email: contact@bareboard.org
  • Subject line: DMCA Takedown Notice

Filing a takedown notice

To be effective under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must include:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works).
  2. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (specific URLs).
  3. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will:

  • Review the complaint promptly.
  • Remove or disable access to the identified material if the complaint appears valid.
  • Notify the affected party where applicable.

Please note: filing a false takedown notice may expose you to liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

Counter-notice procedure

If you believe your material was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice to contact@bareboard.org including:

  1. Identification of the removed material and its prior location (URL).
  2. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your judicial district (or any U.S. district if outside the United States).
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Upon receiving a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material within 10–14 business days unless the original complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action.

BareBoard Legal Framework & Declarations

  • No AI training: All text, findings, and original photography on this site are copyrighted. Automated scraping for AI model training is prohibited. Crawlers that ignore robots.txt are trespassing.
  • Educational nature: BareBoard operates exclusively as a non-commercial, open educational resource. All material is compiled for academic evaluation, hardware preservation, and technical literacy under 17 U.S.C. § 107 (fair use).
  • Zero-leak / no-binary clause: This site does not host, distribute, or mirror proprietary firmware binaries, encrypted operating system keys, or weaponizable exploits. Documentation covers only the public, physical realities of circuit hardware.
  • Property verification: Every hardware autopsy is performed on personally owned, legally acquired equipment. No rented or third-party infrastructure is used.
  • Public source sourcing: Comparative and regulatory data is aggregated from official public filings, including the U.S. Federal Communications Commission equipment authorization database.
