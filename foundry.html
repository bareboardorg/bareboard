Foundry

A layered reference for understanding hardware from the transistor up. Four levels, from silicon to complete systems — each one built on the last.

Level 0 — Silicon

The transistor layer: foundries, lithography nodes, process technology, and the secure elements baked into silicon.

  • Fabs & lithography nodes
  • Secure elements & roots of trust

Level 1 — Interconnect

Board-level documentation: publicly released schematics, board identifiers, bus standards, and repair references.

  • Bus standards & protocols

Level 2 — Controllers

Peripheral and interface controllers: the chips that move data between silicon and systems, and the firmware that runs below the OS.

  • Firmware & flash

Level 3 — Systems

Complete systems where hardware failure has human consequences: medical devices, industrial controllers, avionics. Listed for awareness and lawful research on retired units only.
