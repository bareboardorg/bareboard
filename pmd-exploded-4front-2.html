    Exploded View: Quantum 4Front 2 / 4Front 2 HD

    The whole 4Front 2, taken apart by the people who built it. Quantum’s Illustrated Parts Breakdown for this chair covers two models — the 4Front 2 and the heavier-duty 4Front 2 HD — in one book of 94 pages, exploding 42 assemblies into numbered parts. Every diagram below is Quantum’s own page, followed by the book’s parts table with a plain-language line added for every part. Where the two models differ, the book says so in the part name — the HD suspension packages, the HD drive motors, the HD drive programs, and the HD badge decal are all called out below.

    Coverage: full IPB. Every assembly in the book that carries a parts list is here: 42 assemblies, 460 parts. The book’s last pages are its two appendices — a pages index and a parts index — which carry no new parts and are used here to check the tables, not repeated as steps. The book’s cover plate is a caster-arm diagram with no parts list, so it is not a step either.

    The chair in numbers

    From the owner’s manual and the parts book — the numbers the maker itself prints:

    • Drive: front-wheel drive — the big powered wheels sit at the front, swivelling caster wheels trail at the rear, and small anti-tip wheels sit ahead of the drive wheels. Two motor/brake assemblies, one per side.
    • Motors: Linix motors, 5 mph — or 6.25 mph with the anti-tips drive program (the book’s Q-Logic 3 SD card listings name both speeds).
    • Drive wheels: 14 in × 3 in, flat-free or pneumatic — or 14 in × 4 in with knobby tires.
    • Rear casters: 9 in flat-free wheels, 215 mm tires.
    • Anti-tip wheels: 100 mm, with bearings and spacer.
    • Batteries: two 12 V batteries — Group 24 AGM, 69.2 Ah (C/5), handles removed (MK) — or a Group 24 gel option the book lists as a pair. Always replace both together.
    • Charger: 8 amp offboard CTE charger, with US, UK, EU, and AU power cords listed.
    • Controller: Q-Logic 3, with EX, 3e, and stand-alone joystick options.

    The full specification table — weight capacity, range, turning radius, seat sizes — lives in the product-insert specification sheets the book lists in the Owner’s Package (INFINFB4329 for the 4Front 2, INFINFB4335 for the 4Front 2 HD). Those sheets are not in the library yet, so those numbers are not stated here rather than guessed. If you hold a copy, the library would take it.

    “Do not operate your new power chair for the first time without completely reading and understanding this owner’s manual.”
    “Do not remove the anti-tip wheels.”
    “Place unit on level ground and stand to one side when changing from drive mode to freewheel mode or freewheel mode to drive mode.”
    “The spring tension on your power chair was factory set to meet the needs of the demographic majority of users. Do not adjust the tension of any spring on your power chair.”
    “Do not replace batteries when seat is occupied.”
    “Do not mix old and new batteries. Always replace both batteries at the same time.”
    “Keep tools and other metal objects away from the battery terminals. Contact with tools can cause electrical shock.”
    “Battery posts, terminals, and related accessories contain lead and lead compounds. Wear goggles and gloves when handling batteries and wash hands after handling.”
    “The batteries in your power chair should only be serviced or replaced by a Quantum Rehab Provider or a qualified technician.”
    “CAUTION! Federal law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician or other certified personnel licensed by the law of the State (US only) or region in which this personnel practices to use or order the use of the device.”

    The manual also says there are more warnings in the Consumer Safety Guide (INFMANU5075) that ships with the chair. That guide is not in the library yet; its warnings are not paraphrased here.

    The full record: an open FDA recall

    This chair has an open Class 2 FDA recall, and it matters to every owner, because it is about the chair stopping without warning. The FDA record (recall event 99286, initiated June 25, 2026, posted July 31, 2026, status open and classified) covers the 4Front 2 (recall Z-2841-2026) and the 4Front 2 HD (recall Z-2851-2026); the FDA recall record lists 510(k) K143383 for the recall. The manufacturer’s reason, as the FDA records it: if the “Remind Me Later” option is selected for user reminders, reminder pop-up messages may appear while the wheelchair is in use; if this occurs while driving, the wheelchair may perform an unexpected park brake stop, which may result in fall or injury; if it occurs while not driving, the control system may crash and remain in pre-operational mode. The fix is a software update to Q-Logic version 2.0.13 or later, and owners were told to disable “Remind Me Later” immediately and contact their dealer to schedule the update. Quantity in commerce: 1,102 units for the 2 HD record.

    Sources: FDA recall record Z-2851-2026, preserved copy in this site’s library: recall_Z-2851-2026.html. FDA recall record Z-2841-2026 (4Front 2): FDA accessdata — no library copy held yet.
