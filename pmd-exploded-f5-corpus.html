Exploded View: Permobil F5 Corpus & F5 Corpus VS

This page is different, and the difference is the story. Permobil does not publish an Illustrated Parts Breakdown for the F5 Corpus or the F5 Corpus VS — not for owners, not openly at all. Their user manuals say it in one line: “Spare parts must be ordered through your dealer.” No exploded views, no part numbers, no way to order a caster bearing without a dealer in the middle.

Coverage: manual-only. What Permobil does publish is the labeled overview in each manual — the whole chair, thirteen labeled parts on the F5 Corpus and fourteen on the standing F5 Corpus VS. That is everything the maker gives the person who rides in it. We reproduce both diagrams exactly, and we name the gap exactly: every assembly-level breakdown for these chairs is a source gap until Permobil publishes one. The F5 Corpus VS is the F5 Corpus with a standing function added: same front-wheel-drive layout, plus a chest support and a knee support that hold you steady when the chair stands you up.

The sources

Guide 1: User Manual — Permobil F5 Corpus, Edition 12, 2024-06-05, Order no. 335322 eng-UK
Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): permobil_f5-corpus_owner.pdf

Guide 2: User Manual — Permobil F5 Corpus VS, Edition 17, 2025-04-16, Order no. 335340 eng-US
Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): permobil_f5-corpus-vs_owner.pdf

No IPB, parts catalogue, or service manual for the F5 Corpus or the F5 Corpus VS is published openly by Permobil — their service manuals are written “for technical personnel” and distributed through dealers, and their documentation portal requires a login (verified 2026-11-12). A service manual for the F5 Corpus exists (Edition 3, 2015-06, Order no. 205357-US-0) but is dealer-distributed; no openly published copy is hosted here. If that changes, this page gets the full exploded treatment the same week.

The maker’s own warnings, word for word

Permobil prints these in the F5 Corpus VS user manual. Word for word — including the one that explains why this page cannot give you part numbers:

Spare parts must be ordered through your dealer. Only use spare parts that are authorized by Permobil.

IMPORTANT! Do not modify the wheelchair. Do not modify your wheelchair or any of its components. Your wheelchair has been configured specifically for your needs as prescribed by your healthcare provider. Special skills, training, and knowledge are needed to set up, modify, and repair the wheelchair.

WARNING! Only use the standing function on level ground. Only use the standing function on level ground. If you do not, the wheelchair can lose traction or become less stable and injure you.

WARNING! Do not overload the wheelchair. Do not use the wheelchair to pull any kind of object and never hang excessive weights on the wheelchair. If you do, the wheelchair can become less stable and injure you.

WARNING! Use freewheel mode cautiously. Freewheel mode should only be used on level ground and when an attendant is present to control the wheelchair. If you use freewheel mode on an incline or without an attendant, you can get injured or cause property damage.

The base F5 Corpus manual’s spare-parts rule reads the same way, in British English: “Spare parts must be ordered through Permobil. Only use spare parts that are authorised by Permobil.”

Verbatim from the Permobil F5 Corpus VS User Manual, sections 2.11 and 3; base-manual rule from the Permobil F5 Corpus User Manual, section 2.11.

Build order

The assemblies are laid out in the order you would build the chair: frame first, batteries last. Every step is one labeled diagram from the maker’s guide, followed by its parts table. The Callout column is the label printed on the diagram. There is no part-number column on this page — Permobil publishes no part numbers for these chairs, and we do not invent them.

What is missing, in the open

Frame, drive, seating, and electronics breakdowns for these chairs: not published by the maker. That is not a gap in our research — it is the gate, documented. A service manual for the F5 Corpus exists (Edition 3, 2015-06, Order no. 205357-US-0), but Permobil distributes service manuals through dealers and its documentation portal requires a login, so no openly published copy is hosted here. The serial number label — callout M on the F5 Corpus, callout L on the F5 Corpus VS — is the one key you hold: every part on these chairs is orderable by a dealer using it. Until Permobil publishes the breakdown, a dealer’s parts screen is the only copy — and anything a dealer can see for your chair, you are entitled to ask them to show you.
