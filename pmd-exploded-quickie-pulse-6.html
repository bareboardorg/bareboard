Exploded View: Sunrise Quickie Pulse 6

Sunrise Medical publishes a Complete Parts Manual for the Quickie Pulse 6 — a real parts book, assembly by assembly. The one public copy we could find sits on a dead host that answers every request with 403 Forbidden. So this page is built from what is openly readable: the labeled diagram in the chair’s owner’s manual, “Your Chair and Its Parts,” with every numbered part explained in plain language.

Coverage: manual-only. The diagram covers the Pulse 6 BC, SC, and CC. The parts book (serial prefix PLS) is listed below as a known source gap; when a readable copy is public, this page gets the full treatment.

The source

Guide: Owner’s Manual — Quickie Pulse (6BC, 6SC, 6CC, 5BC, 5CC, 6MPC)
Document: Document 119832 — Sunrise Medical (US) LLC
Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): sunrise_quickie-pulse-6_owner.pdf

This copy was retrieved from a dealer-posted public copy (Open Box Medical, S3). The Pulse 6 Service Manual (111425, Rev. B, 2011) is a troubleshooting guide without parts breakdowns. The Complete Parts Manual for this chair exists — its only located public copy is unreachable, so it is named here as a source gap, not silently skipped.

The maker’s own warnings, word for word

Sunrise prints these on the first page of the Pulse owner’s manual. Word for word:

WARNING — Attention wheelchair users, do not operate this wheelchair without first reading the owner’s manual. If you do not understand the instructions and warnings of this owner’s manual please contact your dealer or qualified technician before operating this Quickie wheelchair. Failure to do so may result in damage and/or injury.

DEALER/TECHNICIAN WARNING — Attention dealers and qualified technicians, do not operate or service this wheelchair without first reading this owners manual. If you do not understand the instructions and warnings of this owners manual please contact the Quickie Technical Service Department before operating and/or servicing this Quickie wheelchair. Failure to do so may result in damage and/or injury.

Verbatim from the Quickie Pulse Owner’s Manual, document 119832.

Build order

The assemblies are laid out in the order you would build the chair: frame first, batteries last. Every step is one exploded view from the maker’s guide, followed by its parts table. The Callout column is the label printed on the diagram. The Part number column is the maker’s own number — the number a parts desk cannot argue with.

  • Frame: Step 1: Your chair and its parts — Pulse 6 BC / SC / CC

Stage 1 — Frame

Start with the skeleton. Every other system bolts to the frame, so it goes together first.

Step 1 — Your chair and its parts — Pulse 6 BC / SC / CC

Part (maker’s label)What this part does
1. Flip-back, height-adjustable armrestSupports your arm. It flips back and adjusts up or down so you can transfer in and out of the chair.
2. VR2 controller joystick (RNET optional)The hand control you drive with. VR2 is the standard control system on this chair; R-Net was the optional upgrade.
3. Captain’s SeatThe padded seat the rider sits in.
4. Front caster 7"The small front swivel wheel, 7 inches across. Not powered — it follows where the chair turns.
5. Rear stabilizing caster 6"The small rear caster, 6 inches across, that keeps the chair stable.
6. Single plate foot platform (shown); swing-away (optional); 70° hangers; swing-away ELR hangers (optional)The plate your feet rest on. The optional hangers swing away so your feet can reach the floor for transfers; ELR hangers can also lift your legs.
7. Drive wheelsThe big powered wheels. The motors turn these.
8. Brake releaseReleases the brakes so the chair can be pushed by hand.
9. Caster fork (front)Holds the front caster wheel and lets it swivel.
10. Caster fork (rear)Holds the rear caster wheel and lets it swivel.
11. ShroudThe plastic cover over the base. It protects the parts underneath.

What is missing, in the open

The full parts book for this chair: published by Sunrise, currently not openly downloadable from anywhere we can reach. The owner’s manual diagram above is everything the maker gives owners today.
