Exploded View: Quantum Q6 Edge 4

The whole Q6 Edge 4, taken apart by the people who built it. Quantum’s Illustrated Parts Breakdown for this chair runs 258 pages and explodes 110 assemblies into numbered parts. Every diagram below is Quantum’s own page, followed by the book’s parts table with a plain-language line added for every part.

Coverage: full IPB. Every assembly in the book that carries a parts list is here: 110 assemblies, 1,025 parts. Four pages of the book are wiring diagrams and a configuration chart — they carry no parts lists, so they live in the linked book only; they are named in the source note below.

The source

Guide: Quantum Rehab Illustrated Parts Breakdown for the Q6 Edge® 4 power chair.

Document: INFMANU5556 Rev. B — 258 pages, Australian edition (the book’s own footer reads “Q6 Edge® 4 (S/N JH7) IPB, AU”; the chargers are listed per region, and the decal package is the AU set).

Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): quantum_q6-edge-4_parts.pdf

Two kinds of book pages are not steps here, because they carry no parts lists: the QLNES configuration chart (book page 87), the PTO QBC wiring configurations (book page 173), and the iLevel® suspension-lock “Y” harness wiring diagrams (book pages 231 and 237). The book also prints 46 “web link” rows inside its tables — pointers to separate Quantum breakdowns for joystick shrouds, mounts, and knobs. Those rows are cross-references, not parts, and are not counted in the 1,025. A few parts in the book have no part number yet — the book prints them as “(COMING SOON)” or “(NO LONGER AVAILABLE)”; their rows are here with an em dash where the number will go. The diagrams carry the book’s own rule, printed on the plates: “Only parts with callouts are available individually. For parts without callouts, the complete assembly must be selected.”

The maker’s own warnings

This breakdown prints no warning paragraphs. On Quantum’s chairs the safety warnings are decals, and the book lists them as parts in the Decal Package assembly. The warning decals in this book include:

  • N/A · DWR1235L515 · DECAL,WARNING,BATTERY WARNINGS,L-1235-515
  • N/A · DWR1235L521 · DECAL,WARNING,CONTROLLER CORD PLACEMENT,L-1235-521

If a warning decal on your chair is peeling or unreadable, it is a replaceable part — its number is in that table. A warning you cannot read cannot warn you.
