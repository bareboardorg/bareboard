Exploded View: Quantum Q6 Edge 3 Stretto

The whole Q6 Edge 3 Stretto, taken apart by the people who built it. Quantum’s Illustrated Parts Breakdown for this chair runs 140 printed pages and explodes 70 assemblies into numbered parts. Every diagram below is Quantum’s own page, followed by the book’s parts table with a plain-language line added for every part.

Coverage: full IPB. Every assembly in the book that carries a parts list is here: 70 assemblies, 609 parts. The book’s two appendices — a pages index and a parts index — carry no new assemblies; they live in the linked book.

The source

Guide: Quantum Rehab Illustrated Parts Breakdown for the Q6 Edge® 3 Stretto power chair.

Document: INFMANU5120 Rev. B — 152 PDF pages (cover, contents, 140 printed pages, two appendix indexes), Australian edition (the book’s own footer reads “Q6 Edge® 3 Stretto IPB (S/N JF3), AU”; exported Tue Jun 23 2020).

Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): quantum_q6-edge-3-stretto_parts.pdf

Two parts in the book have no part number printed — the power-chair warranty insert sheet and one blank decal — so their rows are here with an em dash where the number would go. The Owner’s Package and Decal Package list parts with no diagram callout; the book marks those “*N/A”, and the tables keep that mark. Nineteen rows are parent assemblies covering a range of callouts (A1–A4 and the like): the book sells the whole assembly under one number, and the table says so in the callout. The front-caster damper rows carry the book’s own note: “***Must Be Replaced In Pairs***”.

The maker’s own warnings

This breakdown prints no warning paragraphs. On Quantum’s chairs the safety warnings are decals, and the book lists them as parts in the Decal Package assembly. The warning decals in this book include:

  • *N/A · DWR1234L957 · DECAL,WARNING,DEFLATE TIRE COMPLETELY,L-1234-957
  • *N/A · DWR1235L521 · DECAL,WARNING,CONTROLLER CORD PLACEMENT,L-1235-521
  • *N/A · DWR1235L515 · DECAL,WARNING,BATTERY WARNINGS,L-1235-515
  • *N/A · DWR1235L512 · DECAL,WARNING,EMI-RFI,20V/M,L-1235-512

If a warning decal on your chair is peeling or unreadable, it is a replaceable part — its number is in that table. A warning you cannot read cannot warn you.
