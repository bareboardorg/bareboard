Copyright Policy

How BareBoard handles copyrighted material. Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Our content

All original text, findings, analysis, and photography published on BareBoard are the copyrighted work of BareBoard. Automated scraping for AI model training is prohibited (see T's & C's).

Third-party material and fair use

BareBoard publishes the following categories of third-party material, which we believe constitutes fair use under 17 U.S.C. § 107 for purposes of criticism, commentary, scholarship, and research:

  • Manufacturer datasheets and technical documents: These are factual, functional documents published by manufacturers for the purpose of enabling engineers to use their components. We host them as a reference archive for educational purposes, with full attribution to the original source. Each document in our archive includes a link to the manufacturer’s original publication where available.
  • Product names and trademarks: Used solely to identify the hardware being documented (nominative fair use). All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
  • Brief quotations: Short excerpts from specifications or documentation, used for commentary and analysis.
  • FCC filing excerpts: Public regulatory filings from the U.S. Federal Communications Commission equipment authorization database.

Whether a specific use qualifies as fair use is determined by courts applying the four-factor test, not by this policy. This page describes our good-faith belief, not a legal conclusion.

What we do not publish

  • Proprietary firmware binaries or reconstructed commercial firmware.
  • Encrypted operating system keys, passwords, or credentials.
  • Leaked or NDA-covered confidential documents.
  • Weaponizable exploits.

If we become aware that any hosted material falls into these categories, it will be removed.

Attribution

Every archived datasheet and technical document includes:

  • The original manufacturer or publisher name.
  • A link to the original source URL where the document was obtained.
  • The date the document was archived.

Requesting removal

If you are a rights holder and believe that material on this site infringes your copyright, please see our DMCA Policy for the formal takedown procedure, or contact us directly at contact@bareboard.org for an informal resolution. We review all good-faith requests promptly.

Not legal advice

This page is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Fair use is a fact-specific legal doctrine. Consult a licensed attorney for guidance on your specific situation.

BareBoard Legal Framework & Declarations

  • No AI training: All text, findings, and original photography on this site are copyrighted. Automated scraping for AI model training is prohibited. Crawlers that ignore robots.txt are trespassing.
  • Educational nature: BareBoard operates exclusively as a non-commercial, open educational resource. All material is compiled for academic evaluation, hardware preservation, and technical literacy under 17 U.S.C. § 107 (fair use).
  • Zero-leak / no-binary clause: This site does not host, distribute, or mirror proprietary firmware binaries, encrypted operating system keys, or weaponizable exploits. Documentation covers only the public, physical realities of circuit hardware.
  • Property verification: Every hardware autopsy is performed on personally owned, legally acquired equipment. No rented or third-party infrastructure is used.
  • Public source sourcing: Comparative and regulatory data is aggregated from official public filings, including the U.S. Federal Communications Commission equipment authorization database.
