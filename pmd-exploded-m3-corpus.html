Exploded View: Permobil M3 Corpus

This page is different, and the difference is the story. Permobil does not publish an Illustrated Parts Breakdown for the M3 Corpus — not for owners, not openly at all. Their user manual says it in one line: “Spare parts must be ordered through Permobil.” No exploded views, no part numbers, no way to order a caster bearing without a dealer in the middle.

Coverage: manual-only. What Permobil does publish is the labeled overview below, from the user manual — the whole chair, eleven labeled parts. That is everything the maker gives the person who rides in it. We reproduce it exactly, and we name the gap exactly: every assembly-level breakdown for this chair is a source gap until Permobil publishes one.

The source

Guide: User Manual — Permobil M3 Corpus
Document: Permobil AB (edition held in this site’s library)
Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): permobil_m3-corpus_owner.pdf

No IPB, parts catalogue, or service manual for the M3 Corpus is published openly by Permobil — their service manuals are written “for technical personnel” and distributed through dealers. If that changes, this page gets the full exploded treatment the same week.

The maker’s own warnings, word for word

Permobil prints these in the M3 Corpus user manual. Word for word — including the one that explains why this page cannot give you part numbers:

Spare parts must be ordered through Permobil. Only use spare parts that are authorized by Permobil.

IMPORTANT! Do not modify the wheelchair. Do not modify your wheelchair or any of its components. Your wheelchair has been configured specifically for your needs as prescribed by your healthcare provider. Special skills, training, and knowledge are needed to set up, modify, and repair the wheelchair.

WARNING! Do not overload the wheelchair. Do not use the wheelchair to pull any kind of object and never hang excessive weights on the wheelchair. If you do, the wheelchair can become less stable and injure you.

WARNING! Use freewheel mode cautiously. Freewheel mode should only be used on level ground and when an attendant is present to control the wheelchair. If you use freewheel mode on an incline or without an attendant, you can get injured or cause property damage.

Verbatim from the Permobil M3 Corpus User Manual, sections 2.11 and 3.

Build order

The assemblies are laid out in the order you would build the chair: frame first, batteries last. Every step is one exploded view from the maker’s guide, followed by its parts table. The Callout column is the label printed on the diagram. The Part number column is the maker’s own number — the number a parts desk cannot argue with.

  • Frame: Step 1: The whole chair, labeled by Permobil

Stage 1 — Frame

Start with the skeleton. Every other system bolts to the frame, so it goes together first.

What is missing, in the open

Frame, drive, seating, and electronics breakdowns for this chair: not published by the maker. That is not a gap in our research — it is the gate, documented. The serial number label (callout I) is the one key you hold: every part on this chair is orderable by a dealer using it. Until Permobil publishes the breakdown, a dealer’s parts screen is the only copy — and anything a dealer can see for your chair, you are entitled to ask them to show you.
