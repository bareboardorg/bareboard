The Registry

Every important document, ranked by honesty. A document's value to a repairer is not proportional to how hard a company guards it — so we say exactly what exists, what is public, and what is sealed.

Status legend

  • 🟢 PUBLIC — the vendor publishes it. Collect, preserve, cite.
  • 🔵 PUBLIC-RESEARCH — no vendor doc, but public research exists. We compile originals from it.
  • 🟠 SEALED (NDA) — vendor-internal. Listed for awareness, with public-research pointers. No bounties.
  • ⛔ EXCLUDED — material that exists only to defeat hardware security. Never a target.

Level legend (The Foundry)

  • L0 — silicon (chips, secure elements) · L1 — interconnect (boards, power rails)
  • L2 — controllers (storage, wireless, firmware) · L3 — systems (devices, human impact)

Bounty rules

  1. ✅ Bounties for devices to teardown and document.
  2. ✅ Bounties for rare public documents — collect and preserve.
  3. ✅ Bounties for original documentation written from public research.
  4. ❌ No bounties for sealed documents — that is a market for stolen trade secrets.
  5. ❌ Never list circumvention material.
