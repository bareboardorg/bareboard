How we preserve

  • Vendor-first sourcing: manufacturer sites, then distributors, then official service docs.
  • Integrity: SHA-256 recorded at preservation time; verify any copy.
  • Plain-text mirrors: every document also exists as Markdown where possible.
  • License noted for every item; original docs are CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • No leaked or NDA material — see the content policy.
BareBoard — free forever. No paywall. No ads. No tracking. Verified findings only, uncertainty dated and marked.
