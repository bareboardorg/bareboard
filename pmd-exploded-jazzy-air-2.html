Exploded View: Pride Jazzy Air 2

The whole Jazzy Air 2, taken apart by the people who built it. Pride Mobility publishes an Illustrated Parts Breakdown (IPB) for this chair — the dealer document that shows every assembly exploded into its parts, each part numbered. This page reproduces all 18 assemblies in build order, with Pride’s own part numbers and a plain-language line for every part.

Coverage: full IPB. Every assembly in the guide is here, down to the decal package. The prices printed in this export are Australian list prices, so they are not reproduced — the part numbers are the point. With a part number, you order the $6 part, not the $300 assembly.

The source

Guide: Illustrated Parts Breakdown (IPB) — Jazzy Air 2
Document: INFMANU4980, Rev. A / 100218 — Pride Mobility Products Corporation
Full guide (PDF, in this site’s library): pride_jazzy-air-2_ipb.pdf

Pride posts its IPBs openly; this copy was retrieved from a dealer-posted public copy (Allied Medical, New Zealand). The owner’s manual quoted below is also in the library: pride_jazzy-air-2_owner.pdf (INFMANU4922, Rev. D).

The maker’s own warnings, word for word

Pride prints these in the owner’s manual. They are reproduced exactly — including the parts that tell you a technician must do the work. Read them, then decide for yourself:

WARNING! An authorized Pride Provider or a qualified technician must perform the initial setup of this power chair and must perform all of the procedures in this manual.

WARNING! Do not use the power chair while the drive motors are disengaged! Do not disengage the drive motors when the power chair is on an incline or decline, as the unit could roll on its own. Only engage the freewheel mode when on a level surface.

WARNING! It is important to remember that when your power chair is in freewheel mode, the braking system is disengaged.

WARNING! Avoid applying excessive force to the manual freewheel levers.

WARNING! When faced with an emergency situation, the batteries can be quickly disconnected by pressing the manual shut-off button on the circuit breaker. See figure 2. Use caution. Be advised that turning off power to the power chair may cause the power chair to stop abruptly. To release the emergency stop and restore driving capability to the power chair, follow the circuit breaker reset instructions.

Verbatim from the Jazzy Air 2 Owner’s Manual, INFMANU4922, Rev. D — also in the library below.
