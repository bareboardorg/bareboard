Privacy Policy

We collect almost nothing. Here is exactly what that means. Last updated: October 7, 2026.

What we collect

BareBoard is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require accounts, do not sell or share personal data — because we do not have it to sell. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views; it sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

Information you provide voluntarily

  • Email correspondence: If you email contact@bareboard.org (for takedown requests, corrections, tips, or questions), we receive your email address and message content. We use this solely to respond. We do not add you to any mailing list.
  • Donations: Donations are processed by third-party providers (Ko-fi, PayPal). We do not receive or store your payment details. Their privacy policies apply to those transactions.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: Our hosting provider may log standard web server data (IP address, requested URL, user agent, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. We do not use this data for tracking or profiling.
  • No cookies: BareBoard does not set tracking cookies. No third-party advertising or social media scripts are loaded.
  • Web analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page views. It does not use cookies, does not collect personal identifiers, and does not track individual visitors across sessions or sites. It records only aggregate data such as pages viewed, referring sites, device type, browser, and country.
  • No fingerprinting: We do not attempt to identify or track individual visitors across sessions.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use personal data for marketing.
  • We do not run A/B tests or behavioral analytics on visitors.

Data retention

Email correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry. Server logs are subject to our hosting provider’s retention policy.

Your rights

If you have emailed us and wish to have your correspondence deleted, contact contact@bareboard.org and we will comply.

Children

BareBoard is a general-audience educational resource. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices. The “last updated” date above will be revised accordingly.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact@bareboard.org.

BareBoard Legal Framework & Declarations

  • No AI training: All text, findings, and original photography on this site are copyrighted. Automated scraping for AI model training is prohibited. Crawlers that ignore robots.txt are trespassing.
  • Educational nature: BareBoard operates exclusively as a non-commercial, open educational resource. All material is compiled for academic evaluation, hardware preservation, and technical literacy under 17 U.S.C. § 107 (fair use).
  • Zero-leak / no-binary clause: This site does not host, distribute, or mirror proprietary firmware binaries, encrypted operating system keys, or weaponizable exploits. Documentation covers only the public, physical realities of circuit hardware.
  • Property verification: Every hardware autopsy is performed on personally owned, legally acquired equipment. No rented or third-party infrastructure is used.
  • Public source sourcing: Comparative and regulatory data is aggregated from official public filings, including the U.S. Federal Communications Commission equipment authorization database.
