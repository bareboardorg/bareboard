Rare Manuals

150 of the rarest lawfully-public hardware manuals, datasheets, and schematics — 1980 to today, ranked by how hard each one is to obtain. Public sources only: no leaked documents, no NDA material, no login-walled content.

Preservation: every manual on this page is being archived to the BareBoard collection on the Internet Archive. Documentation links point to archive.org copies, never to manufacturer sites or aggregators.

1980–1989 (20 entries)

#1 Intellivision Keyboard Component Plans (June 2, 1982) only-known-copy

Manufacturer: Mattel Electronics

Internal planning documents for the infamous unreleased Intellivision keyboard add-on — the expansion module whose delays and high price became a running industry story. From the Dr. David P. Chandler collection.

Why rare: Planning documents for a cancelled peripheral from a dead division of a defunct-era Mattel Electronics; survives as a single archive.org scan of a collector's copy.

Public source

#2 Intellivision Intellivoice Service Manual (Model 3330) few-mirrors

Manufacturer: Mattel Electronics (Model 3330)

Service manual for the Intellivoice voice-synthesis module: schematics, PCB layout, component-level repair procedures for the speech cartridge add-on.

Why rare: Mattel Electronics-era Intellivision hardware documentation is thinly preserved; the Intellivoice service manual is found on one document aggregator.

Public source

#3 Osborne 1 Technical Manual (1982) few-mirrors

Manufacturer: Osborne Computer Corporation

Technical manual for the Osborne 1, the first mass-produced portable computer: theory of operation, full schematics (main logic board drawings IAI0063, sheets 1–9), disk electronics board schematics, bill of materials. Osborne folded in 1983.

Why rare: Osborne Computer Corporation is long defunct and its documentation survives almost entirely through community preservation; this scan is the working reference for Osborne 1 repair.

Public source

#4 Kaypro Technical Manual (1984, 1484-C) few-mirrors

Manufacturer: Non Linear Systems / Kaypro Corporation

Kaypro technical manual covering the Kaypro 2/4/10 and 2/83–4/84 series: mainboard removal, scope-waveform troubleshooting, Dotronix and Elston video board adjustments, full schematics and parts lists. Kaypro is defunct.

Why rare: Kaypro (Non Linear Systems) collapsed in the early 1990s; its technical manuals survive on a handful of retro-computing mirrors.

Public source

#5 Sinclair ZX Spectrum Servicing Manual (March 1984, Thorn EMI Datatech) few-mirrors

Manufacturer: Sinclair Research

Official dealer service manual for the ZX Spectrum: fault diagnosis procedures, Issue 2 and Issue 3 board schematics, power-supply and ULA test points. Produced by Thorn EMI Datatech.

Why rare: Sinclair's dealer-only service documentation is far scarcer than its user manuals; this March 1984 Thorn EMI edition lives on obscure personal hosting rather than the big archives.

Public source

#6 Amiga 1000 Assembly Level Repair (1985) several-mirrors
